# Story first

A reel that is cut well but says nothing still fails. Before any cut, agree on the story.

## The one line
Propose it in one sentence: **who** (the viewer, or the person on camera), **what changes** (before → after,
problem → result), and **why the viewer should care**. Example: "A freelancer shows that one command turns a raw
clip into captions that follow every word, so you stop syncing them by hand." Wait for the person to correct it;
their correction is the brief.

## Tell it in the order it happened
Viewers follow a thread: the moment something went wrong, what was tried, what worked, the result. Keep the
real order; do not reorder takes to make it look smarter than it was. Something that happens once (a real
reaction, a real result) is gold — use it as recorded.

## The hook (first second)
- Says or shows the payoff, not the setup. Max 6 words on screen.
- No intros, logos or greetings. If the best line is in the middle of the recording, it can open the reel only
  if the person agrees.

## The scene table
Fill `plan.md` row by row: time, what is said (from the transcript), what is seen, effect, tool. One effect per
row at most, and only if it serves the idea (a zoom on the word that matters, not a zoom every 3 s). The person
approves the table before you edit.

## Honesty
Never put on screen a number, a time ("in 10 minutes") or a result that did not happen. Do not promise what the
product does not do. If a claim needs proof, show the proof or drop the claim.
