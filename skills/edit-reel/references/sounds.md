# Sound effects and music

This skill ships **no audio files**: most free libraries allow use in your videos but not redistribution in a
repository. Download your own and keep them in a folder of your project (for example `sounds/`):

- **Pixabay** (pixabay.com/sound-effects and /music): free for commercial use, no attribution required. Read
  the current licence on the site before using.
- **YouTube Audio Library**: free music and effects; some tracks require attribution.
- Your platform's in-app library for trending audio — add it in the app, not inside the MP4.

Typical set: a short whoosh for slides, a soft pop for things appearing, a click for UI moments. Mix effects
under the voice and music at 10–15 %, then measure the final mix with `reel.py review`.
