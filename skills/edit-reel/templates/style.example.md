# My reel style (example — rewrite it in your own words)

This file is how the assistant learns your taste. Keep it short. Every time you correct the same thing twice,
add a line. Save it as `style.md` in your project folder (or your home folder to use it everywhere).

## Hook
- The whole idea in the first frame, big text, 6 words max. No intros, no logos, no "hey guys".

## Pace
- No pause longer than 0.3 s. If a line is repeated, keep the last take.
- Something new on screen every 2–3 s; if the shot does not change for 4–6 s, a slow zoom.
- Length: memes 10–15 s; talking reels 20–45 s.

## Captions
- 1–2 words at a time, centred, below the chin. Never at the bottom (the app's buttons live there).
- Key word in an accent colour. Perfect spelling; names written right.

## Graphics
- Real screen captures, not mock-ups. Short slide-in, soft sound.
- Nothing on top of anything else. The face is always free.

## Sound
- Clean voice at -14 LUFS, peaks under -1 dB. Quiet instrumental music under the whole reel (10–15 %).
- Trending audio is added in the app, not inside the MP4.

## Never
- Emojis in big on-screen text. Flashy transitions (spins, cubes, flashes).
- Numbers, times or results that did not happen.
